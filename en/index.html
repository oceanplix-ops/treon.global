Industrial partnership questions
What does the local partner provide?

The partner finances machines and the site and organises feedstock, operations and local sales. TREON coordinates the country partnership; MeSentia is responsible for technology and technical release.

What investment is required?

The indicative range is USD 750,000-1,000,000 per MEON pallet machine. Budget the site, infrastructure, permits and working capital separately. The project-specific quotation defines binding scope and pricing.

Which feedstocks are suitable?

Qualified recycled thermoplastics and suitable agricultural fibres, particularly bagasse, are planned. Composition, contaminants, preparation and product quality require confirmation through testing.

When can production start?

After documented technical release, secured rights, financing and site preparation. The timeline and scope are agreed for each project.

How does cooperation begin?

Introduce your company, target country, potential site, feedstock and timeline. TREON then assesses suitability and next steps. An enquiry does not grant territory rights or exclusivity.
