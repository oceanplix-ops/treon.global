W2P · WASTE TO PRODUCT

How W2P puts suitable recovered materials to work

W2P is intended to form suitable secondary feedstock directly into fibre-composite components. Materials, formulation and process must be qualified.

Material qualification and process details

Mixed or contaminated plastic streams are not always economically suited to established recycling routes. W2P (Waste to Product) aims to turn suitable fractions into fibre-composite materials and shaped industrial components.

A thermoplastic matrix binds reinforcing fibres. Ribs and hollow sections use geometry to produce lightweight, stiff components. Ocean Material describes the approach as developed with MeSentia AG and inspire AG in the ETH ecosystem; Ocean Material connects feedstock sourcing with application development.

Decentralised processing

The concept supports processing suitable regional material streams.

06

Fibre-composite lightweight structures

Ribs and hollow sections create stiffness through component geometry.

Preparation is matched to feedstock and formulation. Coarse contaminants must be removed and unsuitable fractions excluded. This is not a process for unrestricted contamination; clean mono-material streams remain valuable for established recycling routes.

Applications and validation

CILOPA as the first application

Pallets provide a practical application for MEON. Dimensions, load capacity, handling-system compatibility and economics will be assessed through documented tests and pilot customers.

Wood-replacement target

CILOPA is being developed as a durable alternative for selected logistics applications where moisture resistance and repeated use matter.

Target: pooling and reuse

Standardised pallet formats support repeated handling, traceable cycles and planned recovery within defined customer systems.

Evidence before scale

Dimensions, loads, equipment compatibility, return routes and total economics must be validated with pilot customers before scale-up.

MEON X and Generation Carbon

Generation Carbon leads the planned integration of residual biomass, process heat and biochar. Electricity generation remains a separately assessed option. MeSentia coordinates the MEON interface. TREON handles commercial incorporation into the franchise package.
