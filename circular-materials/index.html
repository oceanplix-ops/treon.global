Circular materials from recycled plastics for Asia

Circular value begins with the material. Recovered plastics and agricultural residues can be qualified, compounded and transformed into durable industrial applications.

From concept to industrial execution

Recovered plastics

Mixed recovered plastics are an underused industrial resource. The CIRPAL concept is designed to make practical use of different recycled polymer streams.

Agricultural fibre residues

Rice husks, straw, sugarcane bagasse, coconut fibre and other regional residues can provide a complementary feedstock stream for composite applications.

Qualified material rather than waste

The commercial challenge is to convert variable waste streams into reliable material specifications for consistent industrial production.

Material qualification for circular composite pallets

The route from recovered material to a repeatable industrial product requires controlled sorting, formulation and validation. TREON coordinates market and partner requirements while specialist technology and production partners define the technical work needed for each feedstock and application.

Feedstock consistency

Incoming recycled plastics and natural-fibre residues must be assessed for composition, moisture and contamination before they are assigned to a formulation.

Formulation and testing

Material recipes, processing parameters and product performance need documented trials before a circular composite pallet can move towards industrial deployment.

Regional supply chains

Local recovery, processing and manufacturing can shorten transport routes and support circular-material value chains in Thailand, Singapore and other Asian markets.

For recycled-plastic pallets in Asia, the practical objective is not simply to use waste: it is to create a qualified material stream that can support reliable production, repeated use and an organised return into future material cycles.
